Adaptations: neutral upper-body presentation, clipping below the torso, recolored materials, simplified lighting, camera animation, and illustrative breathing motion. Original GLB mesh files are unchanged. The software-rendered fallback uses simplified, vertex-clustered derivatives of these meshes. These are reference anatomical models derived from the public Visible Human reference collection, not a visitor's patient scan or an output of the portfolio author's research models. The animation is an illustrative presentation, not a physiological simulation.
