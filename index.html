02 / DISAGREEMENT-AWARE LEARNING
Bounded Stage A evaluatedPRIVATE REPOSITORY · 27 SEP 2026

ConsenDxFormer (A Disagreement-Aware CNN–Transformer Framework for Reliable Lung Nodule Assessment)

A 2D CNN–Transformer study of radiologist-assessment distributions on annotation-informed lung-nodule crops.

183nodules in primary test set
99patients in primary test set
3seeds for baseline evaluation
Multi-rater supervisionPatient-disjoint splitsCalibration & explanations
Research scope & current status +

Study design

Multi-rater ordinal-distribution supervision, disagreement weighting, spatial attention alignment, and cross-scale fusion. Evaluation includes baselines, component ablations, calibration, uncertainty, and explanation analyses.

Findings & limits

Findings do not establish overall classification superiority or improved average calibration; explanation results are mixed. Labels represent radiologist assessments, not pathology-confirmed diagnoses. Full-scan detection, 3D segmentation, and clinical validation are outside this study.

Stage A implementation and bounded evaluation are complete. Stage B has not started. Manuscript preparation has not begun.

Research in progress. No peer-reviewed publications to date. Project status: October 2026.

HOW I APPROACH RESEARCH

Performance is
only part of
the picture.

01

Preserve disagreement

Study differences between expert assessments rather than treating every label as unambiguous ground truth.

02

Evaluate with care

Use patient-aware splits, baseline comparisons, and controlled ablations to keep conclusions grounded.

03

Make limits visible

Examine calibration, uncertainty, explanations, and failure cases alongside predictive performance.
